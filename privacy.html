Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Zen Video Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Zen Ed Paradise. It is used solely to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

The app requests the youtube.upload permission through Google OAuth in order to upload videos to the authorized channel. It does not read, collect, or store any other Google account data.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's private automation environment and are used exclusively to upload the owner's videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
